SIGAS (Sistema de Gestión de Atención de Salud) is a web-based platform built for health center administrators to manage the full lifecycle of patient care — from scheduling appointments and reserving clinical boxes to tracking mammography referrals and auditing every action taken in the system. Whether you're coordinating a single box or overseeing an entire day's schedule across five clinical areas, SIGAS gives you the tools to do it from one place.

## How to get started

Follow these four steps to go from first login to managing your first patient appointment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to SIGAS">
    Open the SIGAS URL in your browser. Enter your administrator RUT (e.g., `12345678-9`) and your password, then click **Ingresar**. You'll be redirected to the dashboard automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the dashboard">
    The dashboard shows today's **Daily Box Occupancy** grid — all five clinical boxes laid out side by side with time slots from 08:00 to 17:00. Color-coded blocks tell you which appointments are booked and which slots are free at a glance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule a patient">
    Click **+ Agendar Paciente** in the top-right corner of the dashboard. Fill in the patient's RUT, full name, care type, and preferred date and time. The system automatically assigns the correct clinical box based on the care type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage referrals">
    Navigate to **Mamografías** in the top menu to view, register, and follow up on mammography referrals for your patients.
  </Step>
</Steps>
