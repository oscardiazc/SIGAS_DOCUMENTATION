The Administrators module lets you control who can log in to and operate SIGAS. Every person who schedules appointments, manages patient records, or accesses the audit log must have an administrator account. You can create accounts for new staff, update credentials when details change, and remove access promptly when staff leave. All account activity — including the creation, editing, and deletion of other administrators — is recorded in the audit log.

## Accessing the Module

Navigate to **Panel Admin → Administradores** from the main sidebar.

---

## Administrator Account Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **RUT** | Chilean national identity number — the unique identifier for each administrator account (format: `12345678-9`) |
| **Full name** (`nombre`) | The administrator's name as it appears in audit log entries and the interface |
| **Email address** (`correo`) | The administrator's email address for reference |
| **Password** | Bcrypt-hashed password used to log in and to authorise sensitive operations (deletions) |
| **Profile photo** | Optional circular photo displayed in the administrators list and the header |

---

## Adding an Administrator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Panel Admin → Administradores">
    Open the administrators list from the main sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + Nuevo Administrador">
    Click the **+ Nuevo Administrador** button in the top-right corner. The creation form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the account details">
    Fill in the new administrator's **RUT** (format: `12345678-9`), **full name**, and **email address**. RUT and name are required; email is optional but recommended.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an initial password">
    Enter a temporary password in the **Contraseña** field. The new administrator should change it after their first login. SIGAS does not enforce password expiry, so communicate the change requirement directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a profile photo (optional)">
    Click **Choose File** under Foto de Perfil and select an image (JPG, PNG, or WebP). Use the circular crop tool to frame the photo, then click **Aplicar Recorte** to confirm. If you skip this step, a placeholder avatar showing the administrator's initial is used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Guardar">
    Click **Guardar** — the account is active immediately. The new administrator can log in straight away using the RUT and password you set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing an Administrator

To update an existing administrator's details, click their row in the administrators table. A profile popup appears showing their name and RUT. Click **Editar** to open the edit form.

In the edit form you can update:

- **Name** and **RUT**
- **Email address**
- **Password** — enter a new password to change it; leave the field **blank** to keep the existing password unchanged
- **Profile photo** — upload a new image to replace the current one

Click **Guardar** to apply changes. The edit action is recorded in the audit log with your RUT and the details of what was changed.

---

## Resetting a Password

There is no self-service password reset from the SIGAS login page. To reset another administrator's password:

1. Open their profile by clicking their row in the administrators table.
2. Click **Editar**.
3. Enter the new password in the **Contraseña** field.
4. Click **Guardar**.

The new password takes effect immediately. Notify the administrator of their new credentials directly.

---

## Deleting an Administrator

<Warning>
  You cannot delete your own account while you are logged in. Another administrator must perform the deletion. Attempting to delete your own account will return an error.
</Warning>

To delete another administrator's account:

1. Click their row in the administrators table to open their profile popup.
2. Click **Eliminar**.
3. Enter **your own** administrator password in the confirmation field to authorise the deletion.
4. Click **Confirmar Baja**.

The account is removed immediately and the page reloads. The deletion is recorded in the audit log.

## Security Best Practices

<Tip>
  Use a unique password for each administrator account. Shared passwords make it impossible to trace which individual performed a given action in the audit log.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Review the audit log periodically to monitor administrator activity — especially scheduling deletions, patient removals, and account changes. Filter by action type to focus your review.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Remove administrator accounts promptly when staff leave the health center. There is no account suspension feature; deletion is the only way to revoke access.
</Tip>

<Note>
  All administrator actions — scheduling appointments, deleting records, editing specialist or patient data — are recorded in the audit log with the administrator's RUT and full name. This provides a complete, tamper-evident record of who did what and when.
</Note>
