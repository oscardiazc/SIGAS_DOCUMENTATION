The Patients module gives you a complete directory of every person registered in your health center. From a single screen you can search the directory, inspect a patient's full appointment history across all care types, review their contact and insurance details, and — when necessary — permanently remove their record from the system. Patient entries are created automatically the first time an appointment is scheduled for a given RUT, so the directory always reflects your actual scheduling activity.

## Accessing the Module

Navigate to **Panel Admin → Pacientes** from the main sidebar.

---

## Patient Record Fields

SIGAS stores the following information for each patient:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **RUT** | Chilean national identity number — the unique identifier for every patient record (e.g., `12345678-9`) |
| **Folio** | Optional external reference number used by some health centers for internal tracking |
| **First name** (`nombre`) | Patient's given name |
| **Last name** (`apellidos`) | Patient's surname(s) |
| **Phone number** (`telefono`) | Contact phone, captured at the time of scheduling |
| **Email address** (`correo`) | Optional email contact |
| **Section / Unit** (`seccion_unidad`) | The organizational unit or department the patient belongs to |
| **Insurance type** (`previsión`) | The patient's healthcare coverage, one of: FONASA A, FONASA B, FONASA C, FONASA D, ISAPRE, CAPREDENA, DIPRECA, or PARTICULAR |

---

## Searching for a Patient

The left-hand panel on the Pacientes page contains the patient directory and a live search bar. As you type, the list filters instantly — no need to press Enter or click a button.

You can search by any of the following:

- **RUT** — full or partial (e.g., `1234` matches all RUTs containing that sequence)
- **Full name** — first name, last name, or both
- **Folio** — if the patient has an external folio assigned
- **Care type** — the type of consultation the patient has previously attended

Click any name in the filtered list to load that patient's complete profile and appointment history in the right-hand panel.

---

## Viewing Appointment History

When you select a patient from the directory, the right panel displays:

- **Personal details** — name, RUT, folio, phone, email, unit, and insurance type
- **Distribution chart** — a doughnut chart showing the breakdown of all appointments and referrals by care type
- **Appointment table** — a full chronological list of every recorded attendance, including:
  - Date and time range
  - Box number
  - Care type (shown as a colour-coded badge)
  - Attending specialist
  - Modality: **Normal**, **Sobrecupo** (overbooking), or **Derivación** (mammography referral)

<Note>
  Mammography referrals appear in the history as **Derivación externa** — they do not have a box time slot but are counted in the care-type distribution chart.
</Note>

## Editing Patient Data

Patient records in SIGAS are updated automatically whenever a new appointment is scheduled using the same RUT — the system always writes the most recent contact details provided at scheduling time.

If you need to correct a phone number, email address, unit assignment, or insurance type outside of a new appointment, you can update those fields directly through the patient record in the directory. Click the patient's name, then use the edit action available in their profile panel.

## Deleting a Patient Record

<Warning>
  Deleting a patient removes their record and all associated appointment history permanently. This action is logged in the audit trail but cannot be reversed.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Panel Admin → Pacientes">
    Open the patient directory from the main sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the patient">
    Use the search bar to find the patient by RUT, folio, or name. Click their name to load their profile in the right panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Eliminar Paciente">
    In the top-right corner of the patient profile panel, click the red **Eliminar Paciente** button. A confirmation modal will appear showing the patient's full name and RUT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your administrator password">
    Type your current administrator password in the **Ingrese su Contraseña de Administrador** field to authorise the deletion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Confirmar Eliminación">
    Click the **Confirmar Eliminación** button. The patient, all their appointment records, and all associated mammography referrals are immediately and permanently removed. The directory reloads automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Patients created through the Box Reservation feature (internal room bookings) use a system-generated RUT starting with `SALA-` and are not linked to real patient records. They appear in the directory but represent room reservations, not clinical patients.
</Note>
