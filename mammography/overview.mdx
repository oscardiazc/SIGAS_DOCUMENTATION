The Mamografías module gives you a centralized view of every external mammography referral your health center has created. Because mammography exams take place outside your facility, SIGAS tracks them in a dedicated referral list — completely separate from the clinical box schedule — so you always know where each patient stands in the screening process.

## What is a Mammography Referral?

A mammography referral is an external referral: the patient is sent to an outside facility for their mammography exam, which means **no clinical box at your health center is occupied**. The referral does not block a time slot or assign a specialist. Instead, SIGAS creates a standalone record that you can monitor from start to confirmed appointment.

This separation keeps your internal schedule clean while ensuring that referred patients are never lost in the process. Every referral moves through a three-stage workflow so your team always knows what action — if any — is still required.

## Referral Statuses

Each mammography referral is assigned one of three statuses. Update the status as the referral progresses to keep the tracker accurate.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="⏳ Lista de Espera" icon="clock" href="/mammography/referral-workflow">
    The referral has been created and the patient is waiting. No contact with the external center has been made yet. This is the default status when a referral is first saved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="📩 Solicitada" icon="envelope" href="/mammography/referral-workflow">
    The referral has been formally requested to the external center. Your team has communicated the need, but an appointment date has not yet been confirmed.

  <Card title="✅ Agendada" icon="calendar-check" href="/mammography/referral-workflow">
    The mammography appointment has been confirmed at the external facility. The patient has a scheduled date for their exam.

</CardGroup>

## The Mamografías Module

Access the Mamografías module by clicking **Mamografías** in the top navigation bar. The page is divided into two areas:

**Summary Cards** — Three cards at the top of the page display a live count of referrals in each status:

- **Lista de Espera** — total referrals awaiting action (shown with a yellow accent)
- **Solicitadas** — total referrals formally requested (shown with a blue accent)
- **Agendadas** — total referrals with a confirmed appointment (shown with a green accent)

**Referral Table** — Below the summary cards, a full table lists every referral on record, sorted from most recent to oldest. Each row displays:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Fecha** | Date the referral was created |
| **RUT / Folio** | Patient RUT and optional folio number |
| **Paciente** | Full patient name |
| **Contacto** | Phone number and email address |
| **Unidad / Previsión** | Section or unit and the patient's insurance type |
| **Estado** | Current referral status (editable inline) |

Use the **search bar** in the top-right corner of the table to filter referrals instantly. You can search by:

- Patient **RUT**
- **Folio** number
- Patient **name**

The table updates as you type — no need to press Enter or reload the page.

## Updating a Referral Status

You can change a referral's status directly from the table without opening a separate form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Mamografías">
    Click **Mamografías** in the top navigation bar to open the referral tracker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the patient">
    Use the search bar to filter by RUT, folio, or name — or scroll through the table to locate the patient's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the status dropdown">
    In the **Estado** column of the patient's row, click the dropdown selector. It currently shows the patient's active status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the new status">
    Choose one of the three options:
    - **⏳ Lista de espera**
    - **📩 Solicitada**
    - **✅ Agendada**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the update">
    The status updates instantly. A brief confirmation message — **"Estado guardado."** — appears at the top of the table. No save button is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Every status change is recorded in the audit log so your team can always see when a referral was updated and by whom.
</Note>
