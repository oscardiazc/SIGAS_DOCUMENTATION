SIGAS supports **sobrecupo** (overbooking), which allows you to schedule a patient appointment in a time slot that is already occupied by another appointment in the same box. Under normal conditions, occupied slots are hidden from the time slot dropdown — sobrecupo overrides this filter so that all slots, free and occupied, become selectable. This gives administrators the flexibility to respond to clinical demand without restructuring the existing schedule.

## When to Use Sobrecupo

Sobrecupo is intended for situations where clinical demand exceeds normal scheduling capacity and an additional patient must be seen in the same time window. Common scenarios include:

- **Emergency or urgent walk-in visits** that cannot wait for the next available slot
- **Last-minute arrivals** referred by another unit when no free slots remain for the day
- **Specialist running ahead of schedule** and able to take an additional patient in a slot already technically assigned to another appointment
- **Administrative corrections** where a booking error has left a slot blocked but the physical box is actually free

<Warning>
  Overbooking bypasses the normal availability check. Use it only when clinically or operationally justified. All sobrecupo appointments are flagged in the attendance history and recorded in the audit log with the action code `CITA_SOBRECUPO`.
</Warning>

## Enabling Sobrecupo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the patient scheduling form">
    Click **+ Agendar Paciente** on the dashboard. The standard scheduling form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in all patient data and appointment settings">
    Complete the **Datos del Paciente** section with the patient's RUT, name, phone, section/unit, and previsión. Then select the **Tipo de Atención**, confirm the automatically assigned box, choose the **Especialista**, and set the **Fecha**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the 'Habilitar Sobrecupo' checkbox">
    Locate the **Habilitar Sobrecupo (Permite seleccionar y agendar en horarios ya ocupados)** checkbox, displayed with a red background inside the form. Check it to enable overbooking mode. The **Bloque Horario Disponible** dropdown refreshes immediately and now shows all time slots for the selected box and date — including those already occupied. Occupied slots are displayed in red text and labeled **[Ocupado - SOBRECUPO]** so you can distinguish them from free slots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the desired time slot">
    Choose the time slot from the **Bloque Horario Disponible** dropdown. You may select any slot — free or occupied. The slot label confirms whether it overlaps with an existing appointment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Guardar Cita'">
    Click **Guardar Cita** to save the appointment. The appointment is recorded with the sobrecupo flag and the action is logged as `CITA_SOBRECUPO` in the audit log. You are redirected to the dashboard, where the new appointment block appears in the box column at the selected time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Sobrecupo Appears on the Dashboard

Sobrecupo appointments are visually identical to regular appointments on the dashboard grid — they use the same care-type color and display the patient's name. The sobrecupo flag is recorded at the data level and surfaces in two places:

- **Attendance history** — sobrecupo appointments are marked with a **Sobrecupo** badge so clinical staff can identify that the appointment was booked outside normal capacity rules.
- **Audit log** — the action is stored as `CITA_SOBRECUPO`, including the administrator's identity, the patient's RUT, the box, and the date and time of the appointment.

<Tip>
  Sobrecupo does not physically move or modify existing appointments — both the original appointment and the overbooking appointment coexist independently in the same time slot. Managing the clinical workflow for overlapping appointments is the responsibility of the attending staff.
</Tip>
