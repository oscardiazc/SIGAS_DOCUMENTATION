The SIGAS dashboard is the central workspace for healthcare administrators. Every time you log in, it displays today's full occupancy picture across all five clinical boxes — from the first appointment at 08:00 through the last slot at 17:00. Color-coded blocks let you assess availability, identify appointment types, and act on any slot in seconds.

## Dashboard Layout

The dashboard renders a scrollable day-grid with a fixed time column on the left and one column per clinical box on the right.

**Time column** — Lists hourly labels from 08:00 to 17:00 (with a break at 13:00–14:00 for lunch), anchoring every appointment block to its exact position in the day.

**Box columns** — Each of the five active clinical boxes occupies its own vertical lane:

| Column | Box Name |
|---|---|
| 1 | Box Procedimientos |
| 2 | Box Dental |
| 3 | Box Médico |
| 4 | Box Salud Mental |
| 5 | Box Rehabilitación |

Each appointment block is rendered inside the corresponding box column at the correct vertical position and height. Blocks that span multiple rows represent longer appointments — for example, a 60-minute Dental consultation occupies the full height of one hour.

## Color Legend

Appointment blocks are color-coded by care type so you can identify the nature of each appointment at a glance without opening the detail modal.

| Care Type | Color | Hex Code |
|---|---|---|
| Toma de Muestras | Cyan | `#00BCD4` |
| EMP | Green | `#4CAF50` |
| Dental | Pink | `#E91E63` |
| Morbilidad | Blue | `#2196F3` |
| Certificados | Orange | `#FF9800` |
| Salud Mental USIT | Purple | `#9C27B0` |
| Kinesiología | Teal | `#009688` |
| Reserva de Sala | Dark Gray | `#4A5568` |

The color legend is permanently displayed in a strip below the grid so you can cross-reference it at any time without leaving the dashboard.

## Quick Actions

Two action buttons appear in the top-right corner of the dashboard header:

- **+ Agendar Paciente** — Opens the patient scheduling form. Use this to book a new clinical appointment for a patient in any available box.
- **+ Agendar Box** — Opens the box reservation form. Use this to block a clinical box for internal use without linking the reservation to a patient record.

Both buttons are available at all times, regardless of the current day's occupancy level.

## Viewing Appointment Details

Clicking any colored appointment block on the dashboard grid opens a detail modal titled **Detalle de la Atención**. The modal displays:

- **Patient name and last name**
- **RUT** (Chilean national identification number)
- **Care type** (e.g., Morbilidad, Dental)
- **Start and end time** of the appointment
- **Section / Unit** the patient belongs to

Review this information to confirm the appointment is correct before taking any further action. Close the modal by clicking **Cerrar** when you are done reviewing.

## Deleting an Appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click on the appointment block">
    Locate the appointment block in the dashboard grid and click on it. The block is identified by the care-type color and the patient's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the appointment in the detail modal">
    The **Detalle de la Atención** modal opens automatically. Verify the patient name, RUT, care type, time, and section/unit to ensure you are deleting the correct record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Eliminar Cita'">
    Click the red **Eliminar Cita** button at the bottom of the modal. A password confirmation panel appears below the appointment details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your administrator password">
    Type your administrator account password into the **Contraseña de Administrador** field. This step re-authenticates your identity before the deletion is processed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Confirmar'">
    Click **Confirmar** to complete the deletion. The appointment block disappears from the grid immediately, and the system records the action in the audit log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting an appointment is permanent and cannot be undone. The action is recorded in the audit log under the code `ELIMINAR_CITA`, along with the administrator's identity, the patient's RUT, and the date and time of the deleted appointment.
</Warning>
