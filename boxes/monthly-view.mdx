The monthly box view gives you a bird's-eye picture of appointment activity across an entire calendar month for a single clinical box. Unlike the weekly view's hour-by-hour grid, the monthly view focuses on volume and pattern — letting you see at a glance which weeks are packed, which days are light, and where gaps exist before they become problems.

## Accessing the Monthly View

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a box's weekly view">
    In the top navigation bar, click **Horarios Boxes** and select the box you want to inspect (for example, **Box Médico** or **Box Salud Mental**). This opens the weekly view for that box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to the monthly view">
    In the top-right corner of the weekly view, click **Vista Mensual**. SIGAS loads the monthly calendar for the same box, starting on the current month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the Monthly Calendar

The monthly calendar displays a 7-column grid (Monday through Sunday) with one cell per calendar day. Only confirmed appointments are shown.

- **Day cells** — each cell contains the day number and a scrollable list of appointment entries for that box on that date. Each entry shows the start time and the patient's first name, color-coded by care type.
- **Appointment density** — days with many appointments have taller, fuller cells. Lighter days have few or no visible entries. This makes it easy to scan the month and immediately spot high-demand periods.
- **Today's highlight** — the current day's cell is outlined in blue and labelled **(Hoy)**, so you always have a visual anchor for the present date.
- **Sobrecupo indicator** — overflow appointments display a dashed red border around their color chip, distinguishing them from regular confirmed slots at a glance.
- **Day navigation** — click any day cell to jump directly to the weekly view for the week containing that date, giving you the full hourly detail for that period.

<Note>
  The monthly view is read-only. To schedule or modify appointments, use the dashboard or the scheduling forms.
</Note>

## Navigating Between Months

Use the navigation controls at the top of the monthly view to move through the calendar:

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| **← Mes Anterior** | Go back one month |
| **Mes Actual** | Return to the current month |
| **Mes Siguiente →** | Advance one month |
| **Vista Semanal** | Switch back to the weekly view for this box |

The month name and year (e.g., *Julio 2025*) appear in bold next to the box title so you always know which period is on screen.

## Planning with the Monthly View

The monthly view is most valuable as a planning and coordination tool. Use it to:

- **Identify high-traffic weeks** — scan for days where appointment entries stack up and compare them against your specialist coverage roster to confirm adequate staffing.
- **Plan specialist leave** — review the month before approving leave requests. A quick look at the monthly view for the affected box tells you immediately whether absence on those dates would leave patients without coverage.
- **Schedule maintenance windows** — use **Reserva de Box / Sala** appointments to block time in the monthly view for equipment servicing, cleaning, or room reconfiguration, making those slots visible to all administrators in advance.
- **Anticipate demand spikes** — recurring patterns (e.g., higher Morbilidad volume at the start of winter) become visible across months, helping you pre-schedule additional specialist hours before demand peaks.

<Tip>
  Review the monthly view at the start of each week to anticipate peak demand and ensure adequate specialist coverage.
</Tip>
