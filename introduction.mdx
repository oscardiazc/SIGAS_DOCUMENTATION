SIGAS (Sistema de Gestión de Atención de Salud) is the central platform for managing daily health center operations. It brings together appointment scheduling, clinical box management, mammography referral tracking, and a full audit trail into a single, browser-based application — so administrators spend less time on paperwork and more time supporting patient care.

## What is SIGAS?

SIGAS is a PHP web application designed for health center administrators who need to coordinate patient appointments and manage day-to-day clinical operations. Rather than juggling spreadsheets or paper-based schedules, administrators use SIGAS to view real-time box occupancy, schedule patients into the right care slot, track specialist schedules, and produce a reliable record of everything that happens in the system.

SIGAS is accessed entirely through a web browser — no installation is required on individual workstations. All data is stored centrally, so every administrator with an account sees the same up-to-date information.

## Who uses SIGAS?

SIGAS is designed for **health center administrators** — the staff members responsible for coordinating patient appointments, managing clinical box availability, and maintaining administrative records. A typical SIGAS user logs in each morning to review the day's occupancy grid, schedules incoming patient appointments throughout the day, and uses the admin panel to keep patient and specialist records up to date.

Access to SIGAS requires an administrator account. Accounts are created and managed by a lead administrator from the **Administradores** panel — individual users cannot self-register.

## Care types

SIGAS supports the following appointment types. Each care type has a fixed duration and is assigned to a specific clinical box.

| Care type | Duration | Assigned box |
|---|---|---|
| Toma de Muestras | 10 min | Box Procedimientos |
| EMP | 30 min | Box Procedimientos |
| Dental | 60 min | Box Dental |
| Morbilidad | 20 min | Box Médico |
| Certificados | 10 min | Box Médico |
| Salud Mental USIT | 60 min | Box Salud Mental |
| Kinesiología | 30 min | Box Rehabilitación |
| Reserva de Box / Sala | 60 min | Internal use |

## Clinical boxes

SIGAS manages five clinical boxes. Each box appears as a dedicated column on the daily dashboard and has its own weekly schedule view.

- **Box Procedimientos** — samples and EMP appointments
- **Box Dental** — dental care
- **Box Médico** — morbidity consultations and certificates
- **Box Salud Mental** — mental health (USIT) sessions
- **Box Rehabilitación** — physiotherapy (kinesiología)
